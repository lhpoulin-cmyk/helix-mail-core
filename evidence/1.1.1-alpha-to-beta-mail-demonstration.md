## Independent review

Each demonstration generated a unique non-secret correlation identifier on the
originating client. The originating identity authenticated through trusted TLS,
submitted locally to the approved test receiver, received a correlated local
reply, and retrieved it through trusted IMAPS. Message bodies and identifiers
are not committed.

Cross-credential isolation used a closed cycle: each physical host attempted
its own credential against another required identity, and Admin and Cluster
Admin attempted their credentials against one another. Every attempt was
rejected. Thus every required identity rejected a different participant's real
credential without copying credentials between physical hosts. The canonical
hyphenated `cluster-admin@home.arpa` was used; no underscore identity was
created.

Before STARTTLS, password-bearing PLAIN and LOGIN were absent. Authenticated
external recipients were rejected before DATA. Public SMTP port 25 and
Fastmail remained disabled. Credentials, AUTH payloads, message contents, and
private configuration were excluded from commands, output, evidence, and Git.
